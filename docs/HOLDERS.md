# Two holders, one vote, one exit

On 13 Nov 2026 two accounts held MIRROR at the same time and disagreed about its rules. Everything below happened on `parley-live.testnet` and is recorded, with every transaction, in [deployments/testnet-holders.json](../deployments/testnet-holders.json). `bun scripts/holders-testnet.ts` repeats it (it needs the two accounts' keys) and resumes from that file.

1. **A second holder buys in.** `parley-relay.testnet` paid 0.5 NEAR and received 0.4233 MIRROR shares at 1.181093 NEAR a share ([place_mint](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/54M8cBxJ2QLuDMBJYBBbsWNJSNuSRNvt4BKKBFeNub6H), [filled](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/4DJKKqqNhit9qcZ3zEZWHUWQCmiHN1sueKtSKDnDrVvQ)). New shares were minted; the price did not move, because shares are always sold at the treasury divided by the shares.
2. **The owner proposes a change.** `parley-owner.testnet`, which holds 1.5 shares, proposed adding one sentence to MIRROR's constitution: *"In talk, keep it short."* ([propose](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/AWE3ckotJpu64CbBebq2c74eQxGdUYKfrVhfe9Kbny2x))
3. **The two holders disagree.** The new holder voted no with 0.4233 shares ([vote no](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/ADtbvN2QciZBtCkJTvRAbTkzpLDQUS8W7gynMop11b7M)); the owner voted yes with 1.5 ([vote yes](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/7BmDCyfLMtMsH7WaznZTXHdRYznSut4uMpQ3R1qf4ZEq)). After the 2 minute vote the amendment passed, 1.5 to 0.4233, with the 20% quorum met ([finalize](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/4ZjVruhBfgv46hyEzzN6tHQ6gZLqNgKJAyFU52aQwUHF)).
4. **The dissenter leaves before the new rule plays.** During the 4 minute safety wait, the new holder released its vote and sold every share ([release](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/CBGcx7NDgBJcqtVEEyRxQUfkS88FdwSbpqFbFTS8i3zs), [place_redeem](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/7poWARRtajfGBR4o5JxkHrtNaRApwoRRDTEMyNKdcgaR)). The sale filled at the same 1.181093 NEAR a share, 172 seconds before the change took effect, paying back 0.4999 NEAR; the shares were burned ([filled](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/Eb2fudSrtnhjgGB6SmjcgvTEHqW2hA2Jk9tC8z93xwQM)). Its whole round trip cost 0.001 NEAR of gas.
5. **The rule takes effect.** MIRROR moved from version 1 to version 2 ([enact](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/4ptxxD8JBz2pQLzxxs5dWwiHFDeZiPFUaJXgE2859W5W)).

What this shows: holders can disagree on chain, the majority decides, and a holder who disagrees can sell at a fair price before the new rule plays. What it does not show: real strangers. Both accounts belong to the Parley team.

To check it without the explorer, read the transactions over RPC (`tx` with the hash and the signer above) and the coin with `get_coin {"coin":"mirror"}`: it reports `constitution_version: 2` and the added sentence.
